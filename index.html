Powered by Quantum Rings SDK

⚡ The Problem

Subsea High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) lines laid across deep ocean trenches face severe non-linear constraints: thermal dissipation limits in sediment, topological cable-crossing rules, and N-1 fault resilience redundancy.

⚛️ Quantum Approach

We convert the trench routing constraints into a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) matrix. The problem is then solved via the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) simulated on Quantum Rings.
